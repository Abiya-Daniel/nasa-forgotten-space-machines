# 🚀 NASA's Forgotten Space Machines

**Their signals may have faded, but their discoveries have not.**

An interactive digital museum exploring NASA's abandoned and retired space equipment on the Moon and Mars.

Developed by **Team 0N1X** for the **2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge**.

---

## 🌌 About the Project

Space exploration has left behind several machines and scientific instruments on the Moon and Mars. Although many have completed their missions or stopped functioning, their contributions to science remain valuable.

This project brings their stories together through an interactive platform featuring mission histories, spacecraft information, maps, and scientific discoveries.

## ✨ Features

- 🚀 **Spacecraft Explorer** – Discover historic space missions and equipment.
- 🪐 **3D Models** – Explore spacecraft and their components.
- 📍 **Interactive Maps** – Locate equipment on the Moon and Mars.
- 📖 **Mission Stories** – Learn about mission objectives and journeys.
- ⏳ **Mission Timeline** – Explore important mission events.
- 🔬 **Scientific Discoveries** – Understand their contributions to space research.
- 🎮 **Interactive Experiences** – Explore mission simulations and learning activities.
